# Cornerman — Round Timer & Combo Caller

A boxing round timer that calls out random punch combinations for you, so you can drill without a partner or coach.

---

## Get the App

**Open this link on your phone:**

https://garyreyes.github.io/cornerman/cornerman.html

Open it in **Chrome** (Android) or **Safari** (iPhone) — not inside Instagram, Messenger, or another app's built-in browser. Those often block the voice call-outs.

### Install it like a real app (recommended)

**iPhone (Safari):**
1. Tap the **Share** icon (square with an arrow, bottom of screen)
2. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
3. Tap **Add**

**Android (Chrome):**
1. Tap the **⋮** menu (top right)
2. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**)
3. Tap **Install**

You'll get a **Cornerman** icon on your home screen. Open it from there for a full-screen app experience — no browser bar.

Note: Open it once while connected to Wi-Fi/data so it can save itself for offline use. After that, it works with no signal (airplane mode included).

## Main Screen — Step by Step

This is the first thing you see when you open the app.

1. **Round lights** (the row of small bars at the top): one per round. As you complete rounds, they light up so you can see progress at a glance.
2. **Status badge** (says READY, WORK, REST, or FINISHED): tells you what phase you're currently in.
3. **Big timer**: counts down the current phase — how much time is left in this round or this rest period.
4. **Round counter**: shows which round you're on (e.g. "Round 3 / 10").
5. **Combo card** (the box in the middle): this is where the called-out punch combo appears, both as numbers and as words, in sync with whatever the voice says out loud.
6. **Combos called**: a running count of how many combos have been called so far this session.

### Start
Tap the red **Start** button. The clock begins counting down your first round, and combo call-outs begin a couple of seconds in.

### Pause / Resume
Tap the same button (now labeled **Pause**) to freeze the clock exactly where it is — timer, round count, and everything else stays put. Tap it again (now labeled **Resume**) to pick up from that exact moment.

### What the timer means
- While the badge says **WORK**, the timer counts down your active round, and combos are called out.
- A **double-beep** sounds when 10 seconds are left in a work round — your warning to finish strong.
- A **bell** sounds at the end of every work round and the end of every rest period.
- While the badge says **REST**, the timer counts down your break — no combos are called during rest.
- When all rounds are done, the badge switches to **FINISHED** and a triple bell plays.

### Reset
Tap the circular arrow button (left of Start) at any time to stop everything and return to Round 1, fully reset — as if you'd just opened the app.

## Settings — Step by Step

Tap the gear icon (right of Start) to open Settings. Everything below auto-saves as you change it — there's a **Done** button at the bottom just to close the panel.

### Round
- **Round length**: how long each work round lasts (adjusts in 15-second steps)
- **Rest length**: how long the break is between rounds
- **Rounds**: total number of rounds in the workout

### Combinations (combo length)
- **Shortest combo** / **Longest combo**: sets the range for how many punches can appear in a single called combo. Example: set both to match for a fixed length, or set a range like 1-6 for variety.

### Combo Timing (how often combos are called)
- **Preset chips**: tap one for an instant pace — Fast (3-6s), 4-6s, 7-10s, or Slow (10-20s)
- **Shortest gap** / **Longest gap**: fine-tune the exact range yourself, in seconds, if the presets don't match what you want

### Voice
- **Voice call-outs**: on/off switch for the spoken combos entirely
- **Call out — Number or Word**: choose whether the voice says the punch numbers ("1, 1, 2") or the punch names ("Jab, Jab, Straight")
- **Announcer voice**: pick which of your device's built-in voices reads the call-outs
- **Voice speed**: slider from 0.5x (slow) to 5x (fast) — drag and the number updates live
- **Test voice**: tap to hear a quick preview using your current settings, without starting a round

### Punches (naming and customizing)
Each row in the Punches list represents one punch/strike:
- **Number field** (left circle): fully editable — type any number you want. Numbers don't have to be unique or sequential, and they're independent of the name, so you can mix and match however feels natural to you.
- **Name field** (middle text box): type any name you like, including moves that aren't in the standard list (e.g. "Gazelle Hook"). Spelling matters — the voice reads exactly what's typed, so spell it the way you want it pronounced.
- **Dropdown arrow**: tap it for a quick-fill list of standard terms (Lead Jab, Rear Straight/Cross, Lead/Rear Hook, Lead/Rear Uppercut, Lead/Rear Body Hook, Lead/Rear Body Straight, Overhand). Picking one fills in the name field only — your number stays whatever you set it to.
- **Toggle switch**: turn a punch off to exclude it from random combos without deleting it — useful for drilling only specific numbers (e.g. disable 1, 3, 5 to drill only 2-4-6). Flip it back on anytime.
- **X button**: permanently removes that punch from the list.
- **+ Add punch**: adds a new blank row you can name and number however you like.

## Troubleshooting

- **No voice, but bell works?** You're probably in an in-app browser (Instagram/Messenger/etc.) — reopen the link in Chrome or Safari directly.
- **Settings not saving, or an update not showing up?** Make sure you're using the installed home-screen app or the same browser each time, and that you've opened the app once while online after any update.
