# Cornerman — Round Timer & Combo Caller

A boxing round timer that calls out random punch combinations for you, so you can drill without a partner or coach.

---

Getting the App

**Open this link on your phone:**

https://garyreyes.github.io/cornerman/cornerman.html

Open it in **Chrome** (Android) or **Safari** (iPhone) — not inside Instagram, Messenger, or another app's built-in browser. Those often block the voice call-outs.

### Install it like a real app (recommended)

**iPhone (Safari):**
1. Tap the **Share** icon (square with an arrow, bottom of screen)
2. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
3. Tap **Add**

**Android (Chrome):**
1. Tap the **⋮** menu (top right)
2. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**)
3. Tap **Install**

You'll get a **Cornerman** icon on your home screen. Open it from there for a full-screen app experience — no browser bar.

Note: Open it once while connected to Wi-Fi/data so it can save itself for offline use. After that, it works with no signal (airplane mode included).

---

## How to Use

1. Tap **Start** to begin your round timer
2. Tap **Pause** anytime to stop the clock; tap again to resume
3. Tap **Reset** to start the whole workout over
4. A double-beep warns you at **10 seconds left** in each round
5. A bell marks the start/end of every round

---

## Settings (tap the gear icon)

**Round**
- Round length, rest length, and number of rounds — fully adjustable

**Combinations**
- Set the shortest and longest combo length (e.g. 1–3, or 1–6)

**Combo Timing**
- Control how often combos are called, from fast (3–6s) to slow (10–20s), with quick presets or a custom range

**Voice**
- Turn call-outs on/off
- Choose **Numbers** ("1, 2, 3") or **Punch names** ("Jab, Jab, Straight")
- Pick a device voice, adjust its speed
- Tap **Test voice** to preview

**Punches**
- Rename any punch (e.g. make "7" mean whatever move you want)
- Toggle a punch **off** to drill specific combos only (e.g. disable 1, 3, 5 to drill just 2-4-6) — it stays saved, just excluded from random calls
- Add or remove punches entirely

---

## Troubleshooting

- **No voice, but bell works?** You're probably in an in-app browser (Instagram/Messenger/etc.) — reopen the link in Chrome or Safari directly.
- **Settings not saving?** Make sure you're using the installed home-screen app or the same browser each time.
